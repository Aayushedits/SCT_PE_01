TASK 2: PROMPTING FOR CREATIVITY
Experiment A — Professional Tone
Internship: SkillCraft Technology

PROMPT USED
Generate 3 startup ideas that solve real problems faced by college students in India. Use a professional and business-oriented tone. For each idea, explain the problem statement, proposed solution, target market, competitive advantage, and revenue model. Prioritize feasibility and market relevance for a student founder with a limited budget.

============================================================
STARTUP IDEA 1: CAMPUSLOOP — CAMPUS MARKETPLACE
============================================================

Problem Statement:
College students frequently face high costs for textbooks, calculators, lab equipment, and other academic essentials. At the same time, senior students often have unused items that could benefit others.

Proposed Solution:
Develop a campus-focused online marketplace that enables students to buy, sell, rent, and exchange used academic supplies and student essentials.

Target Market:
Undergraduate students, particularly first-year students, senior students, and students living in hostels or rented accommodation.

Competitive Advantage:
Unlike general online marketplaces, CampusLoop focuses on college communities, enabling localized transactions, easier product discovery, and potential campus-based verification.

Revenue Model:
- Commission on completed transactions.
- Paid featured listings.
- Promotional partnerships with local stationery and printing businesses.

Feasibility:
A student founder can validate the concept using a simple website or online listing form before investing in a full application.

============================================================
STARTUP IDEA 2: SKILLSWAP — PEER-TO-PEER SKILL EXCHANGE
============================================================

Problem Statement:
Many college students want to develop practical skills such as programming, graphic design, video editing, and communication. However, paid courses and professional mentorship can be expensive.

Proposed Solution:
Create a platform where students exchange skills using a credit-based system. For example, a student could teach basic web development in exchange for a video-editing lesson.

Target Market:
College students seeking affordable skill development, peer learning, and opportunities to build practical experience.

Competitive Advantage:
SkillSwap encourages reciprocal learning and makes use of skills already available within college communities, reducing dependence on paid courses.

Revenue Model:
- Premium memberships.
- Paid workshops and expert-led sessions.
- Optional paid mentoring services.

Feasibility:
The initial version can operate through a student community and a basic website, with automated matching introduced as participation grows.

============================================================
STARTUP IDEA 3: MESSMATE — STUDENT MEAL PLANNING
============================================================

Problem Statement:
Students living away from home often struggle to manage food expenses, cooking time, grocery shopping, and balanced meals within a limited budget.

Proposed Solution:
Build a meal-planning platform that recommends affordable weekly meals based on a student's budget, food preferences, available ingredients, and kitchen equipment.

Target Market:
College students living in rented flats, paying guest accommodation, and hostels with limited food options.

Competitive Advantage:
MessMate focuses specifically on student budgets and practical cooking constraints rather than general-purpose meal planning.

Revenue Model:
- Premium personalized meal plans.
- Grocery affiliate commissions.
- Partnerships with local grocery stores.

Feasibility:
A basic version can generate weekly meal plans and shopping lists without requiring food delivery infrastructure or a physical kitchen.
