TASK 2: PROMPTING FOR CREATIVITY
Experiment B — Creative Tone
Internship: SkillCraft Technology

PROMPT USED
Act as a highly creative startup innovator. Generate 3 original and unconventional startup ideas that solve real problems faced by college students in India. Give each idea a memorable name, a surprising solution, a unique selling point, and a practical revenue model. Avoid generic ideas while keeping them achievable for a student founder with a limited budget.

============================================================
STARTUP IDEA 1: CAMPUSLOOP — THE CIRCULAR CAMPUS ECONOMY
============================================================

Problem:
Students spend money on textbooks, calculators, lab equipment, and other essentials they may need for only one semester. Meanwhile, senior students often leave these items unused.

Creative Solution:
Create a campus-wide reuse network. Students can list items for sale, rent them for a semester, or exchange them for something they need.

Unique Selling Point:
A semester-based sharing economy where students discover useful items from their own college community, potentially with college email verification.

Revenue Model:
- Small fees on successful transactions.
- Paid promotional listings.
- Partnerships with local stationery and printing shops.

Low-Budget Launch:
Begin with a simple listing website or a form-based system for one college.

============================================================
STARTUP IDEA 2: SKILLSPARK — THE 30-MINUTE SKILL SWAP
============================================================

Problem:
Students may want to learn practical skills but lack the time or money for long courses. They may also underestimate the useful skills they already possess.

Creative Solution:
A peer-learning exchange where students offer short, focused 30-minute sessions. For example, one student teaches a basic video-editing technique and earns credits to learn Excel, public speaking, or coding from another student.

Unique Selling Point:
Small, low-pressure learning sessions that fit between classes and reward students for sharing what they already know.

Revenue Model:
- Optional premium membership for advanced matching and scheduling.
- Paid group workshops.
- Partnerships with student clubs or training providers.

Low-Budget Launch:
Match students through a form and a campus group before building scheduling features.

============================================================
STARTUP IDEA 3: LEFTOVER LAB — SMART SHARED GROCERY PLANNING
============================================================

Problem:
Students living in shared flats often buy duplicate groceries, forget ingredients, or let food go unused because flatmates plan meals separately.

Creative Solution:
A shared-flat grocery planner where roommates enter what they already have, set a weekly budget, and coordinate a simple meal plan and shopping list. The service highlights ingredients that should be used first.

Unique Selling Point:
It combines shared grocery coordination with a practical “use what we already have” approach designed for students with limited budgets and basic kitchen equipment.

Revenue Model:
- Premium features for shared households.
- Affiliate commissions from grocery services, where available.
- Sponsored placements from local grocery shops.

Low-Budget Launch:
Test the idea with a shared spreadsheet or simple website for a few student flats before developing an app.

============================================================
